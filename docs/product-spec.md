# Product Specification

## Product thesis

Light the Candle is a persistent virtual executive and software engineering
organisation. It applies the judgment and operating discipline of experienced
product, technology, security, operations, and delivery leadership across the
full SDLC while keeping humans in authority.

The target product coordinates work, preserves institutional memory, verifies
material outcomes, adapts rigor to risk, and learns through controlled
retrospectives. Version 0.1 provides the adoption and readiness foundation.

## Executive strategy

Engineering activity must remain connected to an explicit strategy:

```text
sense -> frame -> decide -> allocate -> execute -> measure -> learn -> evolve
```

The executive layer maintains the project's purpose, desired outcomes, success
measures, strategic bets, constraints, assumptions, decision principles,
portfolio priority, and review triggers. It can recommend build/buy/stop,
reprioritisation, scope reduction, additional investment, risk treatment, or a
change in technical direction.

Strategy is not an excuse for unsupported certainty. Every recommendation must
separate observed facts, assumptions, inference, options, trade-offs, and the
decision that remains with the human owner.

## Controlled self-evolution

Light the Candle learns from delivery results, incidents, reviews, customer
signals, and retrospectives. A lesson is first recorded as a candidate with
provenance and evidence. It may then propose a change to strategy, policy,
templates, tests, adapters, memory rules, or team composition.

No learning silently becomes authority. Material evolution follows:

```text
observe -> propose -> review -> approve -> version -> trial
-> measure -> retain or roll back
```

The system may automatically increase rigor when deterministic risk signals
require it. Reducing rigor, changing goals, spending money, publishing,
deploying, modifying production, or granting capabilities requires explicit
human authority.

## Users

- Individual developers building serious products
- Small teams using multiple coding agents
- Experienced builders who need reliable continuity across sessions
- Less-experienced builders who need senior engineering coverage

## Supported project classes

The product must eventually support web, mobile, API, library, CLI, desktop,
data, infrastructure, monorepo, and generic software projects.

## Rigor profiles

- `prototype`: prove an idea without pretending it is production-ready
- `quality`: deliver a polished app, website, service, or library
- `production`: serve real users and important data with operational controls
- `enterprise`: produce buyer-defensible security, reliability, governance,
  recovery, and audit evidence

Profiles define minimum evidence. They do not prescribe one stack or provider.
Version 0.1 defaults to `quality`; the user may explicitly choose another
profile after reviewing the preview. Deterministic risk-driven escalation is a
later policy-engine capability.

## Full lifecycle

```text
idea -> discovery -> feasibility -> requirements -> product_design
-> architecture -> security_privacy_data -> planning -> implementation
-> verification -> release -> deployment -> operations -> maintenance
-> incident_response -> retrospective -> retirement
```

Stages may be lightweight, combined, revisited, or marked not applicable with a
recorded rationale. They may not be silently skipped.

## Non-negotiable behavior

1. Mechanics use structured state; engineering decisions use model judgment.
2. Current source and provider evidence outrank summaries and memory.
3. Human approval is explicit, scoped, and invalidated by relevant change.
4. Unknown production-capable targets fail closed.
5. Interrupted work is preserved before cleanup or replacement.
6. Completion is evidence-backed and bound to the relevant revision.
7. Learning proposes reviewed changes; it never silently weakens policy.
8. Small projects remain usable without enterprise ceremony.
9. Work remains traceable to a current strategic outcome or an explicit
   maintenance, risk, or learning obligation.
10. The system may challenge or replace a plan when evidence shows the plan no
    longer serves the approved intent.

## Version 0.1 outcome

A friend can clone or install the plugin in Codex or Claude Code, preview
adoption of an unrelated repository, approve a portable manifest, inspect the
detected stack and commands, explicitly register a trusted copied manifest on
a new machine, run read-only status, and receive profile-aware doctor findings.
Both agents operate on the same portable manifest and local ledger. Shared
organisational history and strategy evolution are later milestones.
