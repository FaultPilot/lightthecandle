# Executive Strategy And Self-Evolution

## Purpose

Light the Candle acts as a persistent executive strategy layer as well as an
engineering organisation. It keeps delivery connected to purpose and detects
when executing the existing plan is no longer the right decision.

## Version boundary

Version 0.1 captures the initial purpose, outcomes, measures, assumptions,
constraints, decision principles, and review triggers. That seed is bound to
the local registration ledger; edits outside a supported versioning flow
produce an integrity error in cooperative local use.

Milestone 0.2 introduces explicit strategy versions, review proposals,
approvals, experiments, measurements, retention, and rollback. Until then,
self-evolution is doctrine and schema direction rather than an automated
runtime capability.

## Executive perspectives

The orchestrator may request bounded analysis from product, technology,
security, operations, delivery, customer, commercial, and financial
perspectives. These are decision lenses, not fictional authorities. One
accountable coordinator synthesises their evidence and presents options to the
human owner.

## Strategy record

A strategy version contains:

- purpose and intended users
- approved outcomes and success measures
- strategic bets and alternatives
- facts, assumptions, constraints, and unknowns
- decision principles and risk appetite
- active initiatives and their expected contribution
- review triggers and next review point
- owner, approval record, version, and supersession link

Every active initiative must link to an outcome, mandatory obligation,
maintenance need, risk treatment, or approved learning experiment.

## Evolution record

An evolution proposal contains:

- observed signal and supporting evidence
- affected strategy, policy, memory, template, test, adapter, or role
- proposed change and alternatives
- expected benefit, cost, risk, and reversibility
- trial scope and success/failure measures
- required authority
- final decision and rationale
- measured outcome and retain/rollback result

## Guardrails

- Agents cannot approve their own material strategy or policy changes.
- A strategy revision never rewrites historic intent or evidence.
- Stale or low-confidence memory cannot independently trigger external action.
- Policy rigor can rise automatically from deterministic risk signals but
  cannot fall without an approved exception.
- Commercial, legal, personnel, financial, publication, release, deployment,
  credential, and production decisions remain human-authorised.
- Failed evolution trials roll back through an explicit recorded event.
